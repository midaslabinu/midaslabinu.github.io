This study presents a computational framework for analyzing whole-body coordination patterns in Parkinsonian gait using smartphone-based human pose estimation. By quantifying directional coupling between body landmarks, the proposed approach identifies discriminative movement features that distinguish individuals with Parkinson's disease from healthy controls and demonstrates their potential as digital biomarkers of motor impairment severity.

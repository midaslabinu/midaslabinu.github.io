Heechang Shin received his B.S. degree in Mechatronics Engineering from Incheon National University and is currently pursuing an M.S. degree in Bio-Robot Systems Engineering at MIDAS Lab, Incheon National University. His research focuses on single-cell data analysis and drug discovery using bioinformatics and artificial intelligence. He is particularly interested in graph-based algorithms and the analysis of large-scale biological data.

## Research Interests

- Bioinformatics
- Artificial Intelligence
- Graph-based Algorithms

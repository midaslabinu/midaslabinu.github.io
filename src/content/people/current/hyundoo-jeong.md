Dr. Hyundoo Jeong is an Associate Professor in the Department of Biomedical & Robotics Engineering, College of Engineering, Incheon National University. He leads the Machine Intelligence and Data Science Laboratory (MIDAS Lab).

His research focuses on computational methods for bioinformatics, network biology, single-cell and spatial transcriptomics, healthcare AI, and AI-driven drug discovery.

## Education

- **Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering**, Texas A&M University, College Station, USA, 2017
- **M.S. in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science**, Seoul National University, Seoul, Republic of Korea, 2009
- **B.S. in Electronic and Electrical Engineering**, Inha University, Incheon, Republic of Korea, 2007

## Professional Experience

  Associate Professor, Department of Biomedical & Robotics Engineering, Incheon National University

- **March 2024–August 2025**  
  Assistant Professor, Department of Biomedical & Robotics Engineering, Incheon National University

- **September 2019–February 2024**  
  Assistant Professor, Department of Mechatronics Engineering, Incheon National University

- **September 2018–August 2019**

- **August 2017–August 2018**  
  Postdoctoral Research Associate, Baylor College of Medicine

- **February 2012–June 2012**  
  Patent Search Professional, Patent Information Promotion Center

- **February 2009–March 2011**  
  Software Engineer, Samsung Electronics

## Honors and Awards

- Outstanding Teaching Award, Incheon National University (2022, 2024, 2025)
